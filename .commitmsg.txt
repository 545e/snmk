fix(a11y): use native buttons, and correct the findings the quality gate caught

The SonarQube quality gate evaluated the previous commit and reported 100
new violations on the changed lines. Reviewing them produced better fixes
than the ones in the previous commit.

Accessibility
- The 99 `<div>`/`<span>` elements that reacted to `onclick` are now real
  `<button type="button">` elements. The previous commit made them behave
  like buttons with `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"` and an Enter/Space
  `onkeydown` handler, which fixed the keyboard access but is not what
  `Web:S6819` asks for: a native button is focusable and activated without
  any script, participates in forms, and is announced correctly by
  assistive technology.
- The appearance is preserved by the new `swa-plain` class. Its rules are
  wrapped in `:where()`, which keeps their specificity at zero, so the
  element's own class and its inline styles always win and only the
  browser's default button chrome is removed.
- The 6 `<label>` elements that acted as buttons became buttons too, which
  removes the `tabindex` on a non-interactive element (`Web:S6845`).

Defects the gate pointed at
- `core/js_intelligence.py`: the regex that stripped the quoting characters
  from a value had an ambiguous alternation (`python:S5850`). It is now
  `str.strip("'\"`")`, which is equivalent -- verified over 3 906 samples --
  and has no precedence to misread.
- `core/ai_training.py`, `core/cyber_tools.py`,
  `core/network/discovery.py`: three more unused local variables removed.
- `core/tls_policy.py`: the `NOSONAR` marker was on the line above the
  statement it covers, so the finding was still reported. It is now on the
  statement itself.

Documentation
- `docs/SONARQUBE.md` records the measured before/after numbers and
  explains why native buttons are the right fix.
- `docs/DSA_ENGINEERING_REPORT.md` gains a section on the SonarQube pass.
- `README.md` links the new document.
