fix(audit): close the remaining non-policy findings

Continuing after the audit: the three policy decisions (RBAC, the
authentication/allowlist/approval defaults, and the coverage gate) were left
untouched as requested. This closes the findings that needed no decision.

Real defects:

- SEC-20: the kill-chain report download interpolated ``report_id`` into a
  filesystem path. It is now restricted to an identifier, and the resolved
  path is confirmed to be inside the report directory.
- SEC-23: the orchestrator read the raw ``ai_api_key`` setting, so the
  encrypted ``enc:v1:...`` envelope was sent to the LLM provider as the
  bearer token. The vault read now decrypts it and fails closed when the
  envelope cannot be read.
- SEC-18: the rate limiter was registered inside the auth gate, so a
  request with a bad credential returned 401 before the limiter saw it.
  The limiter is now the outermost middleware; a rejected login consumes
  the budget.
- B101: three ``assert`` invariants in pattern_gating.py and
  signature_matching.py vanished under ``python -O``. They are explicit
  checks now.

Hardening:

- The credential keyfile is created with mode 0600 before the key is
  written, rather than being chmod'ed after an ordinary open.
- The obsolete X-XSS-Protection header was removed; CSP is the mitigation.
- .gitleaks.toml documents the nine known false positives, so a gitleaks
  run now reports zero findings instead of ten.

One finding was withdrawn after verification: the reported mojibake in
.env.example was a PowerShell console artifact, not a file defect.

Tests: 465 passed, 0 failed. 11 new regression tests in
tests/test_post_audit_hardening.py. The reports were updated with the
corrected counts and the corrected location of the B101 findings.
